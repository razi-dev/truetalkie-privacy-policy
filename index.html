Engineered for Mission-Critical Audio

Built with native audio pipelines and zero-retention privacy for teams, events, security, and outdoor operations.

📡

Offline Local Wi-Fi Mesh

Communicate when cellular towers are down. True Talkie broadcasts direct UDP audio over local Wi-Fi, hotspot, and Wi-Fi Direct with zero internet connection.

🌐

Global WebRTC & TURN

Traverse strict NATs and carrier firewalls across 4G and 5G mobile networks with enterprise TURN relay fallbacks and sub-50ms peer signaling.

🛡️

Zero Audio Retention

Your privacy is non-negotiable. Voice packets are streamed live peer-to-peer and never stored, logged, recorded, or uploaded to servers.

⚡

Background Foreground Service

Never miss a transmission. Android Foreground Service keeps the walkie-talkie audio receiver alive even when your phone is locked or you are using other apps.

🎧

Bluetooth & Hardware Headsets

Seamlessly routes live audio to tactical headsets, Bluetooth earpieces, or loud speakerphones with automatic echo-cancellation and noise reduction.

🔋

Battery & Bandwidth Optimized

Engineered with lightweight native modules and wake-lock management to preserve your device battery during 12-hour continuous standby operations.
