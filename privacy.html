Privacy Policy

Application: True Talkie (com.truetalkie.app) Effective Date: October 10, 2026 Compliance: Google Play Data Safety

🛡️ Privacy at a Glance

True Talkie is engineered around a strict Zero-Retention Architecture. We do not record, transcribe, save, or monetize your voice communications. All push-to-talk audio is streamed ephemerally in real-time directly between devices over local UDP mesh or encrypted WebRTC sessions and ceases to exist immediately upon transmission.

1 Information We Collect & How It Is Used

True Talkie is a real-time Push-to-Talk (PTT) communication utility. Our data handling strictly reflects our mission to deliver real-time voice without compromising your privacy:

  • Voice Audio Data: When you press and hold the PTT button, the app accesses your device microphone to capture live audio. The audio is converted into digital streams and transmitted directly to peers on the channel. No audio files, voice clips, or transcriptions are ever saved on device storage or uploaded to persistent servers.
  • Local Display Name & Session IDs: When entering a channel, the app uses a temporary local display name (e.g. "Worker", "Base Station") and generates a randomized session peer ID. These are used strictly to coordinate real-time speaker states within the active session.
  • Channel Signaling Metadata: In internet mode, transient connection metadata (WebRTC SDP offers/answers and ICE candidate addresses) are exchanged to establish direct peer-to-peer audio links. This handshake data is discarded as soon as peers connect.

2 Device Permissions & Purpose

True Talkie requests only the bare minimum Android permissions required for walkie-talkie operation:

🎤 RECORD_AUDIO
Activated only while you hold the Push-To-Talk button to transmit your live voice to channel participants.
⚡ FOREGROUND_SERVICE (MICROPHONE)
Keeps the audio channel listening in the background so you can receive incoming voice calls when your screen is locked.
🔔 POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Displays a persistent status bar notification ("Listening...") ensuring full user transparency when background audio is active.
📡 Wi-Fi & Network Access
Allows discovery and direct UDP socket transmission over local Wi-Fi and mobile hotspots without needing an internet connection.
🎧 BLUETOOTH & Audio Settings
Allows seamless audio routing to Bluetooth tactical headsets, hands-free earpieces, or built-in speakerphones.
🔋 WAKE_LOCK
Prevents system deep sleep during live voice transmissions, avoiding audio dropouts when communicating.

4 Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

When operating in global internet mode, True Talkie coordinates connections using trusted infrastructure partners:

  • Supabase (Realtime Engine): Used exclusively as a signaling switchboard to exchange connection negotiation metadata (SDP handshake and channel presence). Supabase does not handle, relay, or store audio streams.
  • Metered.ca (STUN / TURN Relays): When devices are behind strict cellular carrier NATs (4G/5G mobile towers), encrypted WebRTC packets transit through Metered TURN relays. These relays serve strictly as encrypted transport pipes and do not inspect, decode, or save audio payloads.
  • Google Play Services: Used to check for in-app updates and distribute verified app bundles.

5 Security & Encryption

True Talkie implements industry-standard protocols to guarantee end-to-end communication privacy:

  • WebRTC Encryption: All voice streams traveling over the public internet are encrypted using mandatory DTLS-SRTP (Datagram Transport Layer Security / Secure Real-Time Transport Protocol) standards.
  • Offline Isolation: In local mesh mode, voice packets circulate exclusively across your private Wi-Fi or hotspot subnet and never reach the public internet.
  • Zero-Log Architecture: Our backend systems retain zero logs of who communicated, channel session timestamps, or conversation audio.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

True Talkie does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because our application does not require user registration or profile creation, no personal identities are gathered from any user.

7 User Rights & Data Deletion

Because True Talkie operates on a zero-retention model without storing user accounts or personal data on external servers, there is no personal data stored to request deletion for. You can reset all local app preferences at any time simply by clearing the application storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
