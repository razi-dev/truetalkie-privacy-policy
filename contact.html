Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about True Talkie operation.

How does offline mode work without internet?

True Talkie uses local UDP broadcast sockets on your local network. When devices are connected to the same Wi-Fi router or portable mobile hotspot, audio travels directly peer-to-peer without ever touching the internet.

Why is there a persistent "Listening..." notification?

Android requires a Foreground Service with an active notification to ensure the operating system doesn't put the app to sleep. This lets you receive incoming calls while your phone is in your pocket or the screen is turned off.

Are any of my audio conversations recorded?

No. True Talkie operates on a strict zero-retention architecture. Voice is transmitted as live audio packets and played immediately on connected devices. No audio files or logs are ever stored.

Can I talk over cellular mobile data (4G/5G)?

Yes. When internet is detected, True Talkie automatically engages WebRTC with global TURN relays to traverse carrier firewalls and connect you with peers anywhere in the world.
